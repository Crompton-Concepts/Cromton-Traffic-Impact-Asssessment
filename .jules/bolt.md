## 2024-06-17 - [Optimizing Haversine Distance Calculation]
**Learning:** In JavaScript mathematical optimization, replace division with multiplication by a reciprocal (e.g., `value * 0.5` instead of `value / 2`) within performance-critical loops to reduce CPU cycles. In addition, when computing the Haversine distance, pre-calculating the radians conversion constant (`Math.PI / 180`) and caching the results of `Math.sin` for the deltas significantly reduces the overhead of trigonometric calculations. The haversineDistance function is called thousands of times in filtering spatial datasets in `app.js`.
**Action:** Always look for repetitive constant mathematical calculations in inner loops (especially trigonometric operations and division) and hoist or cache them to squeeze out extra performance.
