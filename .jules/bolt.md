## 2025-02-27 - Optimize Haversine Math
**Learning:** In highly mathematical Javascript loops like the spatial mapping engine in this application, the standard Haversine implementation mapping directly to `Math.atan2` contains unnecessary computation. `Math.asin(Math.sqrt(a))` combined with Earth's diameter is mathematically equivalent but saves cycles by bypassing complex arc-tangent branches and avoiding calculating `Math.sqrt(1-a)`. Replacing division by two with multiplication by `0.5` further trims cycles in the inner loop iteration.
**Action:** When implementing or refining spatial algorithms handling large datasets (e.g., GeoJSON parsing in frontend loops), combine constants at initialization and default to multiplicative inverses over division. Favor simpler trigonometric derivations that evaluate to the same geometric bounds.
