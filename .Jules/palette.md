
## 2025-01-20 - Required Field Indicators in Forms
**Learning:** Adding visual indicators (like asterisks) to required fields in forms provides immediate feedback to users, reducing friction and form submission errors. Even if HTML5 validation catches the error later, upfront clarity improves perceived usability.
**Action:** Always include a visual indicator (like `<span class="text-red-500">*</span>`) next to the label of any input field that has the `required` attribute.
